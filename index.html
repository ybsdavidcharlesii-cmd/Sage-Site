What We Offer

Care For The Whole Person

Adult & Geriatric Primary Care

Comprehensive healthcare focused on the unique needs of adults and older adults, including routine evaluations, ongoing health maintenance, and management of acute and chronic medical concerns.

Chronic Disease Management

Individualized evaluation and ongoing management of conditions such as hypertension, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol, chronic kidney disease, COPD, thyroid disorders, anemia, arthritis, gout, and other common chronic conditions affecting adults and older adults.

Preventive Care & Wellness

Age-appropriate health screenings, routine examinations, health risk assessments, immunization review, and lifestyle counseling to help you catch concerns early.

Medication Management

Medication reconciliation and ongoing review to promote safe, effective treatment while helping reduce medication interactions, duplication, and other related concerns.

Transitional & Post-Hospital Care

Follow-up after hospitalization, rehabilitation, or other transitions of care to review medications, address ongoing medical needs, and help reduce the risk of complications and unnecessary hospital readmissions.

Patient & Family Education

Education to help you and your family understand diagnoses, medications, treatment options, and preventive health recommendations, so you're equipped to participate in your care.

Concierge & Private-Pay Care

Care That Fits Your Schedule

Looking for healthcare that fits your schedule and individual needs? Sage Advanced Health Care offers personalized private-pay and concierge services designed to make accessing quality care easier and more convenient.

Ongoing Coordination

Wellness & Family Support

Health and wellness consultations, plus family and caregiver consultations to keep everyone involved in your care aligned.

Meet Your Provider

Gregorie Acceus

Adult-Gerontology Primary Care Nurse Practitioner

Gregorie Acceus is an Adult-Gerontology Primary Care Nurse Practitioner dedicated to providing compassionate, evidence-based care to adult and geriatric patients.

With extensive nursing experience across a variety of healthcare settings, Gregorie brings a comprehensive and patient-centered approach to primary care. Clinical experience includes caring for medically complex adults and older adults, chronic disease management, medication management, transitional care, post-acute care, long-term care, and preventive health.

Gregorie believes that healthcare should be a partnership between the patient and provider. Patients are encouraged to ask questions, understand their treatment options, and participate actively in decisions regarding their health.

Our mission: Provide knowledgeable, respectful, and compassionate care that helps every patient live as safely, independently, and healthfully as possible.

Important Information

Please Read Before Contacting Us

Emergency Notice

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.

Do not use this website, email, contact forms, messaging services, or a patient portal to communicate an emergency or other time-sensitive medical concern. Electronic communications may not be reviewed immediately.

Symptoms such as severe chest pain, significant difficulty breathing, signs of stroke, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, or another potentially life-threatening condition require immediate medical attention. Sage Advanced Health Care does not provide emergency medical services through this website.

Medical Disclaimer

Information presented on this website is provided for general educational and informational purposes only. It is not intended to replace individualized medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.

Use of this website does not, by itself, establish a patient-provider relationship with Sage Advanced Health Care or any of its healthcare professionals. Treatment recommendations are made only after an appropriate clinical evaluation and establishment of a professional healthcare relationship.

Privacy Statement

Sage Advanced Health Care respects the privacy and confidentiality of our patients. Information submitted electronically may be subject to privacy and security limitations. Patients should avoid submitting sensitive medical information through ordinary email or unsecured website forms.

Protected health information will be handled in accordance with applicable federal and state privacy requirements and the practice's Notice of Privacy Practices. A complete Notice of Privacy Practices is available separately to patients as required by applicable law.
